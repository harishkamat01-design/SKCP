## Entity and Attribute

Learned the difference between Business Objects, Database Entities and Attributes.

Example:

Customer

Attributes:

- Customer ID
- Name
- Phone Number
- WhatsApp Number
- Address

Also discovered that Orders and Payments are not attributes of Customer but separate business entities connected through relationships.

---

## Primary Key

Discovered the purpose of a Primary Key through real business examples instead of memorizing a definition.

Examples discussed:

- Customer ID
- Product ID
- Supplier ID
- Order ID
- Payment ID
- Machine ID
- Labour ID

A major realization:

A Primary Key exists because names and phone numbers cannot always uniquely identify business records.
