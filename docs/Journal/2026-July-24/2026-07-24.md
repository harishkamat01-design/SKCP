# Topics Covered

## Business Architecture

Today we shifted our thinking from software architecture to business architecture.

Instead of discussing React, PostgreSQL, APIs or databases, we focused on understanding how the factory actually operates.

---

## Business Domains

The entire factory was divided into three major business domains.

- Raw Materials
- Production
- Sales

This became the foundation of the SKCP Business Operating System.

---

## Business Principles

Several long-term business principles were identified.

- Business First
- Simplicity Before Complexity
- Quality Before Quantity
- Customer Trust Above Short-Term Profit
- Software should mirror the real business

## Business Rules

Discovered the difference between Business Rules, Business Processes and Business Principles.

Examples include:

- Products must complete curing before sale.
- Inventory reduces only after dispatch.
- Every dispatch generates a receipt.
- Payments are tracked against each delivery.

## Weekly Factory Operations

Understood how planned Wednesday power outages are utilized productively.

Activities include:

- Machine servicing
- Mould replacement
- Production area cleanup
- Vehicle loading
- Preventive maintenance

A valuable lesson was that business constraints can become productive opportunities.

---

## Business Resilience

Studied how the business responds to:

- Continuous rain
- Machine breakdown
- Delayed sand deliveries
- Labour shortages

Observed that instead of stopping work completely, alternative business activities are performed such as payment collection, maintenance and market visits.

---

## Customer Relationships

One of today's biggest discoveries was that SKCP is a relationship-driven business rather than a transaction-driven business.

The founder's construction experience helps customers make better decisions instead of simply selling products.

---

## AI Vision

The AI vision evolved significantly today.

Instead of focusing on automation alone, the long-term goal became building a Business Advisor capable of supporting business decisions.

Future examples include:

- Pending payment prioritization
- Production suggestions
- Preventive maintenance reminders
- Purchase recommendations
- Risk identification

---

# Biggest Learnings

Today's session completely changed my understanding of software architecture.

I learned that great software begins with understanding the business rather than selecting technologies.

One of the most important discoveries was that software should preserve the founder's business knowledge instead of simply digitizing manual work.

I also realized that Customer Trust is the real asset of the business and every architectural decision should strengthen it.
