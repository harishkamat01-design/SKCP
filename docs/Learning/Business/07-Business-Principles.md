# Business Principles

Business Principles define the values that guide every decision inside SKCP.

---

## Principle 1

Business First

Software should always support the business.

---

## Principle 2

Simplicity Before Complexity

Simple solutions are preferred over complicated ones.

---

## Principle 3

Quality Before Quantity

Deliver fewer quality products rather than many poor-quality products.

---

## Principle 4

Customer Trust Is Everything

Long-term customer relationships are more valuable than short-term profit.

---

## Principle 5

Preserve Founder Knowledge

Years of practical experience should become part of the software.

---

## Principle 6

Adapt To Constraints

Business should continue operating even during unexpected situations.

Examples:

- Rain
- Labour shortage
- Machine maintenance
- Electricity outage

---

---

## Principle 7

Business Truth Over Convenience

Information should always reflect the actual business situation, even if it requires additional calculations.

The software should never store incorrect or duplicated information simply because it is easier.

Example:

Pending Amount is calculated rather than stored to ensure it always represents the true financial position.

---

## Principle 8

Single Source of Truth

Every important piece of business information should exist in only one place.

Duplicate information eventually creates inconsistent information.

The software should always identify the true owner of every business fact.

---

## Principle 9

Every Decision Should Have Evidence

Business decisions should be based on accurate information rather than memory or assumptions.

The software should help the owner answer questions such as:

- How much stock is available?
- Which customers have pending payments?
- Which products sell the most?
- Which supplier provides materials most frequently?

---

## Principle 10

Business Events Should Never Be Forgotten

Every important activity performed by the business should leave a permanent record.

Examples:

- Customer Order
- Payment
- Delivery
- Production Batch

This preserves the history of the business and supports future analysis.

---

## Principle 11

Good Information Creates Better Decisions

The primary purpose of the software is not to store data.

Its purpose is to transform business information into better business decisions.

The better the information,

the better the decisions.

The better the decisions,

the stronger the business.

---

## Principle 12

Technology Must Respect Existing Business Practices

Technology should improve the way the business operates without removing practices that have been proven successful over many years.

Software should complement experience, not replace it.

---

## Principle 13

Business Growth Should Never Require Rebuilding the System

Every design decision should allow the business to grow naturally.

Future expansion may include:

- More customers
- More products
- More suppliers
- More machines
- More factory locations

The software should support this growth without major redesign.

---

## Principle 14

Understand Before Improving

Before suggesting any improvement,

first understand:

- Why the current process exists.
- What problem it solves.
- What business value it provides.

Only then should improvements be introduced.

---

## Principle 15

Software Is a Business Partner

The software is not just a record-keeping tool.

It is a partner that helps:

- Reduce repetitive work.
- Preserve business knowledge.
- Improve operational visibility.
- Support better decision-making.
- Prepare the business for future AI capabilities.

---

## Principle 16

Architecture Should Reflect Business Values

The architecture should always reinforce the core values of the business.

For SKCP, these values include:

- Quality before quantity.
- Customer trust over short-term profit.
- Accurate information over convenience.
- Simplicity over unnecessary complexity.
- Long-term sustainability over short-term shortcuts.

---
