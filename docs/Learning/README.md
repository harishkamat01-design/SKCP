## Learning Philosophy

SKCP follows a **Business-First Learning Approach**.

Every technical concept is first understood from the business perspective before being implemented in software.

The learning journey follows this sequence:

Business

↓

Architecture

↓

Database

↓

Backend

↓

Frontend

↓

AI

↓

Deployment

This ensures every technical decision supports the actual business.

---

## Key Principles Learned So Far

- Business drives technology.
- Every piece of information has one owner (Data Ownership).
- Normalize until every table owns only its own truth.
- Business Objects naturally become Database Entities.
- Every Business Event becomes a Transaction.
- Master Data changes rarely.
- Transaction Data changes every day.
- Good databases mirror real business operations.
- AI depends on high-quality business data.
- Software should become a business partner, not just a record-keeping tool.

---

## Rule

Every time something meaningful is learned:

1. Understand the business problem.
2. Connect it with software engineering concepts.
3. Apply it to SKCP.
4. Document only what provides long-term value.
5. Reuse the knowledge throughout future modules.

Knowledge written today becomes experience tomorrow.
