Key Realization:

A database is not a collection of tables.

It is a representation of business reality

## 2026-07-28

Today I realized that architecture is not about designing software.

Architecture is about understanding how a business works.

Database tables are simply one representation of business operations.

The biggest shift in my thinking was moving from:

"What column should I add?"

to

"Who owns this information?"

This mindset is helping me think more like a software architect than a database designer.
