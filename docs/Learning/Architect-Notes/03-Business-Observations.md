# Business Observations

---

Observation

The customer never thinks about database tables.

The customer only performs business activities.

Examples:

- Place Order
- Make Payment
- Receive Delivery

Therefore,

Software should model business events rather than technical structures.

---

Observation

Master Data changes rarely.

Transaction Data changes every day.

This observation naturally determines database design.

---

## Inventory

Inventory is the heartbeat of manufacturing.

Two truths exist:

1. Raw Material Stock
2. Finished Goods Stock


## Production

Production does not own inventory.

Production transforms inventory.
