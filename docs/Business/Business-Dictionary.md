# SKCP Business Dictionary

This document contains the real words used inside the factory.

---

## Ettangi Kallu

Solid Cement Block

Variants:

- 4"
- 6"
- 8"

---

## Reti

Sand

---

## Cement Cheela

Cement Bag

---

## Current Gone

Power outage.

Production stops until electricity returns.

---

## Mould

Machine attachment used to manufacture one block size.

Example:

- 4" mould
- 6" mould
- 8" mould

---

## Dina

Days

---

## Trip

One vehicle journey between factory and customer site.

Example:

A Bajaj Ace carrying 200 blocks equals one trip.
